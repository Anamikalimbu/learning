Smart Display Solutions

Pretech International Co., Limited, founded in 2010, specializes in Android and Linux-based smart display solutions for control consoles. We offer fully customized solutions ranging from 3 inches to 100 inches, as well as off-the-shelf options, focusing on technical development, balancing quality and cost, and providing excellent sales service. Our smart panels feature a variety of functions such as 5G, LTE, Wi-Fi, POE, RS485, KNX, Bluetooth, Zigbee, ZWave, Thread, Matter, NFC, 1D TOF, 3D TOF, IR, RF, AI acceleration etc... providing excellent HMI (human-machine interface) experience.

These features are tailored for various industries, such as: home automation, Intercom and security system, In-vehicle infotainment, Smart sport, healthcare, home appliances, advertising Kiosk, payment systems, education and more. Our professional R&D, sales, and service teams have extensive experience, successfully delivering numerous large ODM and OEM custom projects. We focus on hardware and OS development, also including integrated MDM and OTA applications, ensuring high quality and cost-efficiency while providing exceptional service.

Our Story

Who We Are

Pretech International Company Limited was founded in 2010 with a clear mission — to deliver innovative, customizable smart display and control devices to businesses worldwide.

Over the past decade, we've grown from a small R&D team into a global technology company with a state-of-the-art 20,000+ sqm manufacturing facility in Shenzhen, China. Our own brand "Yottafun" was registered in 2016.
